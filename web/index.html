                        How do we figure this out?

                        The short version, in three steps.

                        1. 1We gather a year of reports

                          Each city publishes its crime reports. We use the last 12 months, so older reports roll off as new ones come in.

                        2. 2We split each city into hexagons

                          Each hexagon is about half a mile across. Zoom in and they split into smaller ones, about a fifth of a mile across.

                        3. 3We compare it to its own city

                          0.78×22% below the city average

                          1.0× is the city's average for this hour and day of the week. Below 1 is quieter, above 1 is busier. Cities are never compared with each other.
